Chapter 03

The Open Door

Subject: Davey Scatino · Season 2

1 · The scene

Davey, an old friend who runs a sporting-goods store, keeps asking for a seat at Tony's high-stakes card game. Tony keeps saying no. When he finally says yes, Davey loses more than he can pay, and Tony's crew empties the store from the inside.

2 · The psychology

Psychologists call it reactance: tell people they can't have something, and they want it more. Every no made the table look bigger, and made Davey readier to bet whatever it took to sit at it.

3 · The tell

Access held back until you're at your weakest, then offered as a favor.

4 · In real life

The "exclusive" deal that's closing tonight. The partner who's always just about to commit. The boss who keeps the promotion out of reach until you've stopped asking for a raise.

5 · The counter

Ask why the yes showed up now. If it came exactly when you had the least leverage, it wasn't a favor.
